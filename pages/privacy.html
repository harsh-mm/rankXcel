Privacy Policy · Last updated January 2026

How RankXCL handles your information.

RankXCL ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using rankxcl.net or contacting RankXCL, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — including your name, business name, website URL, email address, phone number, and any details you submit through our contact form, audit form, or email correspondence. We also collect technical information automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages viewed, and session data via standard analytics tools.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to audit requests, contact form submissions, and direct inquiries
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve our local SEO and web design services
  • To send relevant follow-up communications about RankXCL services (you may opt out at any time)
  • To analyze website performance, search activity, and user behavior on rankxcl.net
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Cookies and analytics

RankXCL uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console, to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some site features may not work as intended. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business — including hosting, analytics, email, and CRM platforms — under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of RankXCL, our clients, or others.

5. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including CCPA for California residents), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information; opt out of marketing communications; and request a copy of the data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email contact@rankxcel.net.

7. Security

RankXCL implements reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and notify you of any material breach as required by law.

8. Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites — Google Business Profile, Google Maps, social platforms, partner sites. RankXCL is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

9. Children's privacy

RankXCL services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated via the website or direct notice where appropriate.
